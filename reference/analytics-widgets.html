Analytics widgets ​

Every widget the Analytics page shows and an analytics dashboard can place, listed under its home band: the band of the Analytics page it belongs to, and the group the widget picker lists it under. Each widget follows the scope bar. One that does not follow a test filter (a selection, test tags or browsers) says so under its title when a test filter is set. The numbers the widgets show are defined once, in Metrics.
